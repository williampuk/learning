Wallets need a shared way to find keys again

Alice’s wallet derives many keys. A restored wallet needs to know which branch was used and what output construction each child feeds. BIP86 specifies a convention for single-key P2TR outputs. The first Bitcoin mainnet receiving address is m/86′/0′/0′/0/0. This convention is wallet behavior.

SegmentMeaning
mBIP32 master key. Here m is a path prefix, distinct from the Merkle root symbol.
86′Hardened purpose for single-key P2TR. Purpose 44′ typically means legacy P2PKH and 84′ native P2WPKH for their respective specs.
0′Hardened mainnet Bitcoin coin type.
0′Hardened account zero.
0External receiving branch. 1 is internal/change. Both are non-hardened.
0First address on that branch, also non-hardened.
  1. Derive childFrom the private account derive /0/0, or use the corresponding public account for CKDpub.
  2. NormalizeObtain the child point and take its even-Y representative P.
  3. Apply BIP86TapTweak binds x(P) with the root omitted.
  4. EncodeUse x(Q) in 51 20 <32 bytes> and Bech32m.

BIP86 still uses a tweak even for this one-key policy. BIP341 recommends the no-script commitment as a construction rule. It gives participants a public recipe to check rather than treating a bare aggregate point as proof that no alternate spend is present. Given the seed, Alice derives the hardened account privately, then the child privately, and keeps the private key for signing. The fixture below starts from the published account xpub, reproduces the public branch, and matches the official output.

Derive an address with public data

An account xpub contains a compressed point K and a chain code c. For the non-hardened branch and index, apply CKDpub twice. The 4-byte index serialization is big-endian, so zero is 00000000. The chain code is the HMAC-SHA512 key. The HMAC data is the full 33-byte compressed key plus that index.

I = HMAC-SHA512(c_parent, serP(K_parent) || ser32(index))
I_L = I[0:32];  I_R = I[32:64]
K_child = K_parent + int(I_L)G;  c_child = I_R

These are BIP32’s point and chain code calculations. Reject I_L at least n or a child at infinity and proceed according to BIP32’s invalid-child rules. The child’s raw compressed parity matters while deriving deeper BIP32 children. At the final child only, switch to the x-only even-Y convention. The Taproot tweak is public, so knowledge of the xpub suffices to reconstruct addresses. It gives no tweaked private scalar by itself.

When the seed is only part of the backup

If Alice restores a standard single-key wallet, the mnemonic and any passphrase reconstruct her master key. Matching network, account, receiving/change branches, derivation purpose and address discovery determine whether the restored wallet finds the same outputs. A watch-only descriptor such as tr([fingerprint/86h/0h/0h]xpub/0/*) describes how to recover the public receiving addresses. A signer still needs the secret.

Now consider migrating the vault to new software. Alice restores her seed, but the vault’s internal key came from Alice and Bob’s ordered cooperative keys and the commitment included a specific tree. For address discovery the new coordinator needs the complete policy, or an equivalent derivation and commitment recipe. Recovery for a particular output needs the relevant leaf bytes and proof, obtainable from the whole tree or a saved control block. Retain the descriptor or equivalent policy record, participant xpubs and origins, key order and tweak/aggregation conventions, leaf versions, scripts and tree shape, and enough information to identify UTXOs. Keep recovery key backups and coordinator metadata available to their intended holders. A seed alone cannot infer unrecorded scripts or sibling hashes.

Level 1 · Why does a BIP86 wallet keep using a tweak even without a script tree?

Hint

Compare the x values in the experiment.

Worked solution

BIP86 specifies a key-only TapTweak, hashing x(P) alone and using x(Q) in the program. The convention is reproducible across wallets.

Level 2 · Can the account xpub derive another hardened account?

Hint

Does CKDpub have the input needed for a hardened step?

Worked solution

No. It can derive the non-hardened receiving/change branch and address descendants. A hardened CKD step requires parent private key material.
